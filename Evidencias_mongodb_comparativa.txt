ST1630 — MongoDB (streaming) y comparativa empírica SQL vs NoSQL

=== ARTEFACTOS ===
- queries/mongo_queries.py
- notebooks/comparativa_sql_nosql.ipynb
- Integración Streamlit: pestaña "Streaming en Vivo" (mongo_queries)
- Ingesta Flink: trending_movies, genre_activity, anomaly_alerts → streaming_results

=== CONSULTAS OPERACIONALES (streaming_results) ===
1) q_trending_top_k_latest_window
2) q_genre_most_active_in_recent_windows
3) q_anomaly_alerts_for_user
4) q_recommendations_for_user (gold_user_recommendations)

=== ÍNDICES (ensure_operational_indexes) ===
- trending_movies: (window_end↓, rating_count↓), (movie_id, window_end↓)
- genre_activity: (genre, window_end↓), (window_end↓)
- anomaly_alerts: (user_id, detected_at↓)
- gold_user_recommendations: user_id UNIQUE

=== BENCHMARK (n=10, 2 warm-ups; Docker local) ===
Latencias (ms, media ± σ):
| Lectura por user_id              | 1.46 ± 0.31  | 0.36 ± 0.07  |
| Filtro + SUM por género          | 1.66 ± 0.21  | 1.33 ± 0.15  |
| JOIN recomendaciones ↔ película  | 1.76 ± 0.27  | 0.70 ± 0.14  |
  (columnas: MongoDB | PostgreSQL)

Throughput 20 000 filas:
| MongoDB insert_many   | 102 993 filas/s (σ 13 226) |
| PostgreSQL batch      |  43 598 filas/s (σ  4 577) |
